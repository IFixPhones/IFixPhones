Questions.

Do I need an appointment?

Not at the shop. Walk in any time we're open. For mobile repair, book a time above or call (210) 724-2800.

How long does a repair take?

Most screen repairs take about 10 minutes. Batteries take 15–20 minutes, because we open your phone slowly and carefully to protect the screen.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Incell and Soft OLED screens have a 30-day warranty for defects, like a screen that stops working on its own. It doesn't cover breakage, and we test every screen with you before you leave. Original parts are covered on select phones when our supplier warranties them. Any new cracks, pressure marks, frame damage or liquid voids the warranty. Read our full warranty and repair policy.

Why can't I see prices online?

Part prices change often and depend on your exact model, so we check the current price for you. Send a free quote request or text us and we'll get back to you with a price.

Which screen should I pick?

Incell is the best price. OLED is our best aftermarket screen, for iPhone 13 Pro and newer Pro models, iPhone 17 and iPhone Air (iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max and Air get OLED only, no Incell). Original is a refurbished original or a genuine Apple part, closest to factory. Tap through the comparison above to see the difference. Not sure? Pick "help me choose" and we'll recommend one for your phone.

How much is the mobile trip fee?

It depends on how far you are from the shop. We'll quote it when we text you to confirm, before anything is booked.

Do you fix more than phones?

Yes. iPads, tablets and more, plus soldering, unlocking and data recovery. Call and ask about your device.
