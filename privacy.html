Privacy Policy

UBRINGIFIX INC, doing business as iFixPhones. 9055 Marbach Rd, Suite 105, San Antonio, TX. Effective November 24, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains what information UBRINGIFIX INC, doing business as iFixPhones (“iFixPhones,” “we,” “us”), collects when you visit ifixphones.net, check in for a repair, or get a text from us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, and email if you give it.
  • Repair details: your device model, serial number or IMEI, the problem, repair notes, photos of the device’s condition, and your signature if you sign at check-in.
  • Payment records: what you paid and how. We don’t keep full card numbers.
  • Your texting choice: whether you said yes to text messages, and when.
  • Website data: what you type into our check-in and booking forms, plus basic technical information our spam protection uses to tell people from bots.

How we use it

  • To repair your device and keep a record of the repair and its warranty.
  • To contact you about your repair by call, email, or text if you said yes to texts.
  • To give you receipts and answer your questions.
  • To protect our forms from spam and fraud, and to follow the law.

Text messages (SMS)

  • Optional. We text you only if you check the optional texting box when you check in, or tell our staff yes at the counter. Saying yes isn’t required to get a repair.
  • What we send: repair updates, like a check-in confirmation, status updates, a ready-for-pickup notice, pickup reminders, and a review request after pickup. Message frequency varies, usually 2 to 5 messages per repair. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Stop anytime. Reply STOP to any message to stop texts. Reply HELP for help.
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Our full SMS terms are in our Terms & Conditions.

Who we share it with

  • We don’t sell or rent your information.
  • Service providers that run our systems for us, such as our database host and our text-message provider. They may use your information only to provide those services to us.
  • When the law requires it, for example a valid court order.

How long we keep it

We keep repair records for as long as we need them for warranties, taxes, and business records. If you give us your passcode, we use it only to test your device and remove it from our records when the repair is finished.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to stop texts at any time.
  • Contact us to see, correct, or delete your information. We may need to keep some records the law requires.

Security and children

Only our staff, signed in to our shop account, can see your repair records. Our services aren’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
