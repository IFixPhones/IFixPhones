1. About this policy

This policy covers every repair and part from iFixPhones ("we", "us"), at our shop and through our mobile repair service. "You" means the customer who brings in or books the device.

When you leave a device with us, book a repair, approve a quote, or let a mobile technician start work, you agree to this policy. We'll also ask you to sign a check-in form that refers to it.

Only a written note on your receipt or repair ticket can change these terms for your repair. Spoken promises from any employee don't change this policy.

2. Before your repair

  • Back up your data. Use iCloud, Google, a computer or another backup before you bring your device in. Any repair can cause data loss.
  • Find My and Activation Lock. Some repairs and tests need Find My iPhone or Google account protection turned off. We'll tell you if yours does.
  • Passcode. A passcode helps us fully test your device after the repair. Giving it to us is your choice. If you don't, we may not be able to test everything, and problems we couldn't test aren't our responsibility.
  • Remove extras. Please take off your case, screen protector, SIM card, memory card and any accessories. We aren't responsible for items left with the device.
  • Ownership. You confirm that you own the device or are allowed to have it repaired. We may refuse service on any device we believe is lost, stolen or locked to someone else, and we may cooperate with law enforcement.
  • Minors. Customers under 18 need a parent or guardian to approve the repair.

3. Check-in and existing damage

Before we start, we inspect your device and note its condition, including scratches, dents, cracks, a bent frame, missing screws, signs of earlier repairs and signs of liquid. We may take photos.

Please tell us about everything wrong with the device at check-in, even if it seems unrelated. Our check-in notes are accepted as the device's condition unless you point out something different before we start.

Some damage can't be seen until a device is opened. This includes hidden liquid damage, corrosion, cracked internal parts, a weak or swollen battery, and earlier repairs done with poor parts. If we find any of these, we'll stop and talk to you before going further.

If your device doesn't turn on, or the screen doesn't show anything, we can't test its other functions before the repair. Problems we find after it powers on (camera, Face ID, speakers, charging and so on) were not caused by our repair unless it's clear they were.

4. Risks of repair

Phones are built to be hard to open. Even when a repair is done carefully and correctly, the following can happen. We'll go over the risks that apply to your phone before we start, and you can decide not to go ahead.

Screen risk when opening from the front

On many phones, the screen has to come off to reach the battery and other parts. That includes iPhone 13 and earlier models, iPhone 14 Pro and Pro Max, iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max, and iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. Removing a screen that's glued down can crack it or cause lines, spots or touch problems, especially if the screen is old, already weak or not original.

Some phones, including iPhone 14 and 14 Plus, the iPhone 15 and 16 models, iPhone 17 and iPhone Air, can be opened from the back for some repairs, which lowers this risk. We'll tell you which way your phone opens.

If your original screen is damaged during a careful, properly performed repair, the cost of a replacement screen is your responsibility. We take our time on these repairs for exactly this reason.

Other risks

  • Frame damage. The frames on some newer phones can bend or chip when opened, especially phones that were already dropped.
  • Water resistance. Once a phone is opened, its factory water seal is broken. We may replace the seal, but we can't guarantee water resistance after any repair. Keep repaired phones away from water.
  • Features tied to the original part. Face ID, Touch ID, True Tone, auto-brightness, battery health readings and some camera features may stop working or work differently after certain repairs. This is a manufacturer limit, not a repair defect.
  • Parts notices. Newer iPhones may show a message such as "Unknown Part" or "Unable to verify" in Settings after a screen, battery or camera is replaced. This doesn't mean the repair is faulty.
  • Software. A device may need an update or restore after a repair, which can erase data.
  • Older or damaged devices. Devices that were dropped, bent, wet, or repaired before may fail completely once opened, even when nothing is done wrong.
  • Data loss. See section 13.

6. Quotes, approval and payment

  • Quotes. Part prices change often, so we check the current price for your exact model before quoting. A quote is based on what we know before opening the device.
  • Approval. We don't start work until you approve the quote. If we find more problems or the price changes, we'll contact you first. We won't do extra work or charge more without your approval.
  • Special-order parts. If a part has to be ordered for you, we may ask for a deposit. Once a special-order part is ordered, the deposit isn't refundable.
  • Payment. Payment is due in full when the repair is finished, before the device is returned. Under Texas law, a worker who repairs an item may keep it until the repair is paid for (Texas Property Code section 70.001).
  • Unfinished repairs. If you cancel after we've started, or a repair can't be finished because of hidden damage, you pay only for work already done and parts already installed or ordered. We'll tell you the amount before you leave.

7. Our workmanship

We perform every repair in a good and workmanlike manner, with the care and skill expected of a professional phone repair technician, following accepted repair practices for your device.

If a problem is caused by how we did the repair (for example, a loose connector or a missing screw), and not by the part itself, an existing weakness or new damage, bring the device back with your receipt. We'll inspect it and fix our workmanship at no charge.

8. Limited warranty

This is a limited warranty. It covers only the specific part we installed, and only defects in that part.

  • When a part is covered. We warranty a part only when our wholesale supplier warranties it to us. If the supplier doesn't warranty a part, we can't either.
  • How long. The coverage period depends on the part and is written on your receipt. If your receipt doesn't show a warranty, the part isn't covered.
  • Which parts. Incell screens are never covered. OLED screens and original parts may be covered on select phones, depending on the supplier and the condition of your phone at check-in.
  • Condition of your phone. We may decline to offer a warranty if your phone has a bent or damaged frame, signs of liquid, earlier repairs, or other damage that could affect the new part. We'll tell you before the repair.
  • What's covered. Defects in the part itself under normal use, such as touch that stops responding without any damage, or a display fault that appears without any impact, pressure or liquid.
  • Who's covered. The warranty is for the customer on the receipt and for the same device only. It can't be transferred.

To the extent the law allows, any implied warranties on a covered part (including the implied warranty of merchantability) last only as long as this limited warranty. Some states don't allow limits on how long an implied warranty lasts, so this limit may not apply to you. This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights that vary from state to state.

9. What the warranty doesn't cover

The warranty is void, and doesn't cover the part, if any of the following apply:

  • Any crack, chip or break in the glass or display, even a small one.
  • Lines, spots, black areas, color bleeding or pressure marks on the display. These come from impact or pressure, even when the glass isn't cracked.
  • Any sign of liquid, moisture or corrosion, including from rain, sweat, steam, spills or a drop in water.
  • A bent, dented or damaged frame or housing.
  • Drops, impact, crushing, pressure, heat, or use with damaged chargers or accessories.
  • The device was opened, repaired or modified by anyone other than us after our repair.
  • Our warranty seal or marks were removed, broken or tampered with.
  • Software problems, updates, apps, viruses, jailbreaks or settings.
  • Parts we didn't replace, and problems not related to our repair. For example, a camera or battery problem after a screen repair.
  • Normal wear, including scratches and the normal aging of a battery.
  • Features lost because of manufacturer restrictions, and parts notices in Settings (see section 4).
  • Loss of water resistance.
  • Any Incell part.
  • Any repair of liquid damage or board-level repair (see section 12).

Whether a part was damaged or is defective is decided by our inspection of the device.

10. Making a warranty claim

  1. Bring the device and your receipt to our shop during business hours, within the warranty period on your receipt.
  2. We'll inspect the device. Inspections may take time, and we may need to keep the device.
  3. If the claim is covered, we'll repair or replace the part with the same or an equivalent part, at no charge.
  4. If a replacement part isn't available, we may refund the amount you paid for that part instead.

A replacement part is covered only for the rest of the original warranty period. The warranty doesn't start over.

If the claim isn't covered, we'll explain why and give you a quote to fix it, if you'd like.

11. Incell parts: no warranty

Incell screens are our lowest-cost option, and the price reflects that they come with no warranty.

Incell parts are sold as is. We make no warranty, express or implied, on Incell parts, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

This doesn't change our commitment to install every part with proper workmanship (section 7).

12. Liquid damage and board repair

Liquid damage and board-level repairs (such as soldering) are unpredictable. Corrosion can keep spreading after a repair, and devices can fail later without warning.

  • We can't promise that a liquid-damaged device can be fixed, or that it will keep working after a repair.
  • Liquid damage repairs, board repairs and any device that has had liquid damage have no warranty.
  • Treatment or diagnosis may make a device stop working completely, or may not save your data.
  • We'll tell you about any diagnosis or treatment charge before we start.

  • We don't look through your data. If you give us your passcode, we use it only to test the device's functions after the repair.

We aren't responsible for data on abandoned devices. Devices are wiped before they're recycled.
